# Gyral brand guide

Everything in `dist/` is generated by `pnpm build` from one source module, `scripts/brand.mjs`. Change the
source, rebuild, commit the outputs. Never edit a file in `dist/` by hand.

## The mark

A gyre: a spiral that turns outward from a point in eight quarter-turns, each one wider than the last.
It stands for the loop at the heart of Model-View-Intent — intent feeds the model, the model drives the view,
the view produces new intent — and for that loop growing as an application grows. The name comes from the same
idea: _gyral_, of a gyre, turning.

The four colours travel round the spiral in a fixed order, blue → red → yellow → green → blue, twice. Each
quarter-turn fades from one colour to the next, so the spiral reads as one continuous movement rather than four
blocks.

Construction (in mark units; the masters live in `scripts/brand.mjs`):

| Turn | Arc           | Radius | Colours        |
| ---- | ------------- | ------ | -------------- |
| 1    | 52,50 → 48,46 | 4      | blue → red     |
| 2    | 48,46 → 44,50 | 4      | red → yellow   |
| 3    | 44,50 → 52,58 | 8      | yellow → green |
| 4    | 52,58 → 60,50 | 8      | green → blue   |
| 5    | 60,50 → 46,36 | 14     | blue → red     |
| 6    | 46,36 → 32,50 | 14     | red → yellow   |
| 7    | 32,50 → 54,72 | 22     | yellow → green |
| 8    | 54,72 → 76,50 | 22     | green → blue   |

Stroke width 7, round ends at the centre and at the tail; the turns meet without seams.

### Small mark

Below 24 px the two innermost turns fill in and turn to mud. The small mark drops turns 1 and 2 and thickens the
stroke to 10 (11 at 16 px). `web/favicon.svg` and the 16 px favicon use it; use the same rule anywhere the mark is
under 24 px tall or under 8 mm in print.

## Layouts

| Layout     | Use it for                                                                  | Files                     |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Mark       | Avatars, favicons, app icons, stickers, anywhere the name is already nearby | `dist/mark/`              |
| Horizontal | The default logo: site headers, READMEs, slides, docs                       | `dist/lockup-horizontal/` |
| Stacked    | Square or tall spaces: posters, T-shirt fronts, title slides                | `dist/lockup-stacked/`    |
| Wordmark   | Running text-heavy contexts where the mark would be too busy                | `dist/wordmark/`          |

Each lockup comes in two words:

- **gyral** is the primary name. Use it by default.
- **gyraljs** is secondary, for places where the plain word is ambiguous or taken: package registries, social
  handles, the GitHub organisation, search-heavy contexts.

The wordmark is Sora SemiBold (600) at −0.02 em tracking, outlined to paths, so no font needs to be installed.
Proportions are fixed by the build: in the horizontal lockup the height of the "l" equals 80 % of the mark's
height, the x-height is centred on the mark, and the gap is 28 % of the mark's height; in the stacked lockup the
word is 1.25 × the mark's width, 22 % of the mark's height below it. Don't rebuild a lockup by hand — use the files.

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space on every side equal to **a quarter of the mark's height** (for the wordmark alone, a quarter of
the height of the "l"). The exported files already include it as margin; don't crop it away and don't place other elements inside it.

| Layout     | Smallest on screen             | Smallest in print            |
| ---------- | ------------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Mark       | 16 px (small mark below 24 px) | 6 mm (small mark below 8 mm) |
| Horizontal | 96 px wide                     | 25 mm wide                   |
| Stacked    | 64 px wide                     | 18 mm wide                   |
| Wordmark   | 64 px wide                     | 15 mm wide                   |

## Variants

| Variant                            | Mark                      | Word          | Background                   |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------- | ---------------------------- |
| `on-black`                         | bright gradient           | white         | black                        |
| `on-white`                         | deep gradient             | black         | white                        |
| `transparent-dark`                 | bright gradient           | white         | none — for dark backgrounds  |
| `transparent-light`                | deep gradient             | black         | none — for light backgrounds |
| `flat-on-black`                    | bright, one colour a turn | white         | black                        |
| `flat-on-white`                    | deep, one colour a turn   | black         | white                        |
| `flat-transparent-dark` / `-light` | flat                      | white / black | none                         |
| `mono-white`                       | white                     | white         | none — for dark backgrounds  |
| `mono-black`                       | pure black `#000000`      | black         | none                         |

Prefer the gradient versions on screen. Use **flat** where gradients can't be reproduced (screen print, vinyl,
enamel pins, embroidery in four threads) and **mono** for one-colour jobs (laser engraving, foil, embossing,
single-colour print, watermarks, photos where colour would clash).

## Do

- Use the files in `dist/` as they are.
- Use `on-white`/`transparent-light` on light backgrounds and `on-black`/`transparent-dark` on dark ones.
- Use mono white or mono black on photographs and busy backgrounds.
- Keep the clear space and minimum sizes.

## Don't

- Don't recolour the mark, change the colour order, or swap the bright and deep palettes.
- Don't rotate, mirror, skew, outline, or add shadows, glows or bevels.
- Don't stretch either axis or change the proportions between mark and word.
- Don't set the word in another typeface or retype it from a font; use the outlined files.
- Don't write the name with a capital in the logo ("Gyral" is fine in running text, the logo is always "gyral").
- Don't put the gradient mark on mid-tone or colourful backgrounds where any of its colours vanish — use mono.
- Don't use the mark as a pattern, a bullet, or decoration inside other artwork.

## File naming

```
dist/<layout>/gyral-<layout>[-<word>]-<variant>[-<width>].<ext>
```

- `layout`: `mark`, `lockup-horizontal`, `lockup-stacked`, `wordmark`
- `word`: `gyral` or `gyraljs` (not on the mark)
- `variant`: see the table above (wordmarks have no flat variants — there's no mark to flatten)
- `width`: pixel width, PNG only
- `ext`: `svg` (master for screen), `pdf` (vector master for print), `png` (raster, transparent where the variant
  is)

Web icons live in `dist/web/`, social images in `dist/social/`, and `dist/manifest.json` lists every file with its
pixel size. `dist/contact-sheet.png` shows everything at a glance.

## Merchandise

- **Screen print, vinyl, enamel, patches:** flat versions; one colour per turn means four spot colours plus the
  garment. Specify Pantone from the table above and ask for a proof.
- **One-colour jobs** (a single ink, foil, laser, deboss): mono. Pick white or black to suit the material.
- **Embroidery:** flat or mono only. The stroke must be at least 1.5 mm wide when stitched, which puts the full
  mark at **about 40 mm wide or more**. Smaller than that, digitise from the small mark (turns 3–8 only, stroke 10).
- **Stickers and die-cuts:** gradient or flat `on-black` / `on-white`; cut line 2 mm outside the clear space.
- **Full-colour print (DTG, offset, digital):** the PDF masters, gradient versions.
- Always send the PDF or SVG, never a PNG, to a printer.

You may, without asking:

- use the logos to refer to Gyral — in articles, talks, tutorials, blog posts, books and videos;

  endorsement;
- use them in community material (meetups, conference slides, non-commercial stickers) that follows this guide.

Ask first (open an issue in this repository) to:

- use them in a product name, a company or project logo, a domain name, or a social handle;
- sell anything carrying them;
- modify them in any way.

**Why not an open licence?** Code is better shared freely; marks are not. A logo's only job is to say "this is
Gyral", and a licence that lets anyone change or reuse it would let it say that about things that aren't.
Keeping the artwork reserved and granting broad permission for honest use is what most open-source projects do
(Node.js, Rust, Python), and it costs fair users nothing.
